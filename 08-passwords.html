    Act 04 · Your playbook

    Five habits that scale.

    HABIT 01
    One account, one password.
    Never reuse — especially your work password. Not even a "variation". A breach anywhere should stay there.
    HABIT 02
    Use a password manager.
    Use the one your company provides for work. It generates, stores, and auto-fills — and refuses to fill on fake look-alike sites.
    HABIT 03
    Long passphrase for the few you memorize.
    Your computer login and your password manager: 4+ random words, 16+ characters. Length wins.
    HABIT 04
    Turn on MFA everywhere.
    Email, banking, password manager first. Prefer an authenticator app or passkey over SMS when offered.
    HABIT 05
    Never share it. Ever.
    Not with IT, not with your manager, not with a coworker covering for you. Nobody legitimate will ask.
    Passkeys are coming. Many services now offer passkeys — sign-in with your device's fingerprint, face, or PIN instead of a password. They can't be reused, guessed, or phished onto a fake site. When a trusted service offers one, it's a great upgrade.
    You don't need a better memory. You need a better system.

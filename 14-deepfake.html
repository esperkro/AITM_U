Act 03 · Verify differently

Trust the channel, not the face.

For each request, decide: can you act on it as received, or do you need to verify through a separate, known channel first?

Voice note from your "CEO" on WhatsApp asking you to buy gift cards for a client event
The voice is spot-on
Gift cards + unusual channel + urgency. Voice clones are cheap. Verify via your known company channel — and gift-card requests are a fraud signature on their own.
Video call from "IT" asking you to share your screen and approve a login
Their face matches the IT staff photo
IT never needs you to approve a login for them. Hang up and contact the service desk through the official portal or number.
Your manager, in the recurring team meeting, reminds everyone to submit timesheets
Company meeting tool · scheduled weeks ago
Routine, expected, low-risk, in your normal channel. Verification effort should scale with what's being asked.
Your "child" calls crying from an unknown number: they're in trouble and need money sent now
It sounds exactly like them
A family emergency scam using a cloned voice. Hang up and call your child (or another family member) on their known number. Consider agreeing a family "safe word".
Vendor emails, in their usual friendly style, that their bank details changed
Writing style perfectly matches past emails
AI can mimic writing style from a compromised mailbox. Bank-detail changes always require a callback to a number already on file.

Act 04 · Your playbook

When anyone can be anyone.

RULE 01
Call back on a known number.
For money, credentials, data, or access: end the conversation and contact the person via the directory, not via the message they sent.
RULE 02
Never skip the process.
Dual approvals and payee verification exist for exactly this. "The CEO said to skip it" is a red flag, not an exception.
RULE 03
Secrecy is a signal.
Real confidential deals still involve your manager and normal controls. Being told to keep it from them is the tell.
RULE 04
Use code words at home.
Agree a family safe word for emergencies. A cloned voice won't know it.
RULE 05
Report attempts.
If someone impersonated an executive to you, they're likely trying others. Report it — even if you didn't fall for it.
You can't out-see a deepfake. You can out-process one.

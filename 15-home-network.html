Act 02 · Fix the router

Your router is the front door.

This is a simulated router admin page with the settings found in many homes. Click Fix on each one to see what to change and why. (Real menus vary by brand — the ideas are the same.)

HomeRouter AX · AdminSecurity score: 0/6
Admin password
Current: default (printed on the sticker)
✓ Changed to a long, unique password stored in your password manager. Default credentials are published online for every model.
Firmware
Current: v1.0.4 (2021) · Auto-update OFF
✓ Updated and auto-update turned on. Old router firmware has known, actively exploited vulnerabilities. If your router no longer gets updates, replace it.
Wi-Fi security
Current: WPA (TKIP) · password "smithfamily"
✓ Switched to WPA3 (or WPA2-AES) with a long passphrase. Weak protocols and guessable passwords let neighbours — or anyone parked outside — join.
WPS (push-button setup)
Current: Enabled
✓ Disabled. WPS PIN mode has well-known weaknesses that let attackers recover the Wi-Fi password.
Remote management & UPnP
Current: Remote admin ON · UPnP ON
✓ Remote admin off; UPnP off unless you specifically need it. These can expose the router and devices behind it to the internet.
Guest / IoT network
Current: Not configured · all devices on one network
✓ Smart TV, cameras, speakers, and visitors moved to a separate guest network. If one gets compromised, it can't reach your work laptop.
Router hardened. Six changes, about twenty minutes, and your home network is harder to break into than most. Bonus: write the new admin password in your password manager, not on the sticker.

Act 04 · Your playbook

Your home, hardened.

STEP 01
Change router defaults.
New admin password, WPA3/WPA2-AES with a long Wi-Fi passphrase, WPS and remote admin off.
STEP 02
Update automatically.
Router firmware and every device. Replace routers that no longer get updates.
STEP 03
Separate the gadgets.
Smart devices and guests go on a guest network, away from your work laptop.
STEP 04
Work device = work only.
No family use, no personal software, no personal cloud storage for company files.
STEP 05
Use the VPN.
Connect to company resources the approved way, even at home.
STEP 06
Something weird? Ask IT.
Strange pop-ups, slowness, new browser toolbars, or a router you can't log into — report it.
The office has an IT team. Your living room has you. Twenty minutes makes all the difference.

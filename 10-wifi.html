Most websites now use HTTPS, which protects what you send. That's good news. But public networks still let attackers redirect you to fake login pages, push fake "update" pop-ups, and watch for anything unprotected. And when you travel, the bigger risks are often physical: a screen someone can read, a laptop left at the table.

Act 04 · Your playbook

Travel light, travel safe.

RULE 01
VPN on, every time.
On any network you don't control, connect the company VPN before opening email, files, or apps.
RULE 02
Prefer your own hotspot.
Your phone's password-protected hotspot beats any open public network.
RULE 03
Install nothing from Wi-Fi pages.
No certificates, profiles, apps, or "updates" from captive portals or pop-ups.
RULE 04
Lock it and keep it.
Lock your screen whenever you step away; keep devices on you; use a privacy filter for sensitive work.
RULE 05
Forget networks after use.
Turn off auto-join for public networks so your device doesn't connect to a look-alike later.
RULE 06
Report losses fast.
Lost phone or laptop? Call IT right away so they can lock and wipe it.
You can't see who's running the network. So act as if someone is.
